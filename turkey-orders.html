📅 Processing and Pickup Schedule

  • Processing day: November 23, 2026
  • Expected pickup dates: November 24 or November 25, 2026

🧾 How Payment Works

  1. Pay the $11 non-refundable reservation payment for each turkey when the order is submitted.
  2. After processing, we will calculate your exact total using the turkey’s actual processed weight at $5.00 per pound.
  3. The $10 credit from each reservation payment will be deducted from your final balance. Your remaining balance must be paid in full before your turkey is released for pickup.
