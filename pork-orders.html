🧾 How the Final Cost Works

Customers are responsible for two primary costs:

  1. Hanging-weight cost, paid directly to Roswell Family Farm. Your deposit is applied toward this balance.
  2. Processing and butcher fees, paid directly to the processor.

Processing estimates are provided for planning purposes. The actual butcher bill depends on the hog’s final weight, processor, packaging and your cut selections.

Bacon, smoked hams, sausage links, hot dogs, cheese products, kielbasa, tomahawk racks and other specialty products can materially increase processing costs.

🔍 What Is Hanging Weight?

Hanging weight is the hog’s weight after slaughter but before it is cut and packaged. Final packaged pork is generally around 65–75% of the hanging weight.

The exact take-home amount depends on whether you select bone-in or boneless cuts and how much meat is cured, smoked or made into specialty products.
