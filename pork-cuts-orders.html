Reserve Your Pasture-Raised Pork Cuts

Raised with care through our partnership with Spofford Farm and offered by Roswell Family Farm — clean, flavorful pork cuts ready for your freezer.

📝 Order Instructions

  • Enter the quantity requested for every product.
  • Enter 0 for any product you do not want.
  • Most packages range from approximately 1.5 to 3 pounds, except for roasts and certain specialty products.
  • Add any special requests or preferences in the notes section.

Example order:

• Thick Cut Bone-In Pork Chops: 3 packages
• Loin Roast: 1 package
• Breakfast Sausage Links: 4 pounds
• Notes: Include packages as close to 2 pounds as possible

🔁 After You Submit

  • We’ll confirm what is currently available.
  • We’ll match your requested quantities as closely as possible.
  • Your final total will be based on actual packed weights.
  • Pickup or meet-up arrangements will be confirmed directly with you.
